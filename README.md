# AI Assisted Citizen Services

Presentation material for a Telugu Government Form Intelligence Platform proof of concept.

## Presentation

- [Telugu Government Form Intelligence Platform (PDF)](presentation/Telugu_Government_Form_Intelligence_Platform.pdf)

The 15-slide deck describes an assist-only, policy-controlled approach to Telugu voice and Telugu-English document processing for operator-led form preparation. Existing government applications remain systems of record; operators retain review and submission control.

## Scope Notice

This material is an architecture baseline and local proof-of-concept discussion artifact. It does not claim production deployment, official MeeSeva integration, GIGW/STQC certification, or validated quality/latency outcomes.
